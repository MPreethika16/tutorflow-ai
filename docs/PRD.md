# PRD Updates

## 1. Overview

TutorFlow AI is an AI-assisted assessment platform designed for tuition teachers. It helps teachers create assessments in their own teaching style, evaluate student responses efficiently, and provide consistent feedback while ensuring that the teacher remains in complete control of all academic decisions.

Instead of generating generic AI questions, the platform learns the teacher's assessment style and generates draft assessments that align with their preferred wording, difficulty level, and question patterns.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Tuition teachers spend a significant amount of time preparing assessments and evaluating student answers.

The current workflow is largely manual:

* Teachers prepare question papers based on their own teaching style and the needs of their students.
* Every assessment is created manually.
* Students complete the assessment.
* Every answer sheet is manually evaluated.
* Feedback is manually prepared.

As the number of students and assessments grows, this process becomes repetitive, time-consuming, and difficult to scale.

---

## 4. Product Goals

The product should help teachers:

* Generate assessment drafts that match their own teaching and assessment style.
* Reduce assessment creation time without sacrificing quality.
* Reduce grading time using AI-assisted evaluation.
* Produce consistent assessments appropriate for the student's grade and learning level.
* Allow teachers to spend more time teaching instead of performing repetitive assessment tasks.

---

## 5. Product Principles

The product follows these principles:

* AI assists the teacher rather than replacing them.
* Every generated assessment reflects the teacher's style rather than generic AI output.
* Teachers always remain responsible for academic decisions.
* AI recommendations are transparent and reviewable.
* Human approval is required before assessments or grades are published.

---

## 6. User Stories

### Teacher

* As a teacher, I want the system to learn my assessment style so future question papers feel like ones I would create myself.
* As a teacher, I want AI to generate a draft assessment based on the topics I have taught, so I spend less time preparing exams.
* As a teacher, I want to review and edit every generated assessment before publishing it.
* As a teacher, I want AI to evaluate student submissions using a rubric so I can grade more efficiently.
* As a teacher, I want to review AI-generated marks before publishing results.

### Student

* As a student, I want assessments that match what was taught in class and my learning level.
* As a student, I want to answer assessments using MCQs, typed responses, or voice responses.
* As a student, I want to review my voice transcript before submitting it.
* As a student, I want to receive timely marks and meaningful feedback after my teacher approves the results.

---

## 8. MVP Scope (Version 1)

### Included

* Teacher authentication
* Student authentication
* Student management
* Assessment creation
* AI-generated assessment drafts
* Teacher review and editing
* MCQ submissions
* Typed answer submissions
* Voice answer submissions
* Speech-to-text with transcript confirmation
* AI-assisted grading
* Teacher approval workflow
* Student results

---

## 10. Non-Goals

Version 1 will not:

* Replace the teacher in creating or grading assessments.
* Publish grades automatically.
* Function as an AI tutor or chatbot.
* Create complete lesson plans or teaching material.
* Support handwritten mathematics grading.
* Include attendance management.
* Include fee management.
* Include a parent portal.
* Include WhatsApp integration.
* Replace a full Learning Management System (LMS).

---

## 11. Success Metrics

The product will be considered successful if it:

* Reduces assessment creation time.
* Reduces grading time.
* Produces assessment drafts that require minimal editing by the teacher.
* Produces consistent and helpful student feedback.
* Reduces the percentage of AI grading overrides over time.
* Achieves high teacher satisfaction with both assessment generation and grading workflows.

---

## Product Vision

Enable every tuition teacher to create assessments in their own teaching style and evaluate them efficiently with AI assistance, while ensuring that every important academic decision remains under the teacher's control.
