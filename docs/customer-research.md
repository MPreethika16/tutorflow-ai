# Customer Profile

The customer is a tuition teacher who teaches students after returning from school. The tuition currently serves approximately 15 students from **Class 3 to Class 10**.

The teacher is responsible for planning lessons, preparing assessments, conducting exams, evaluating student responses, and providing feedback.

---

# Current Workflow

```text
Teach Lesson
      ↓
Prepare Question Paper Manually
      ↓
Conduct Assessment
      ↓
Collect Student Responses
      ↓
Evaluate Answers Manually
      ↓
Provide Feedback
```

---

# Observations

During the interview, the following observations were made:

* Question papers are prepared manually.
* Every teacher has their own preferred assessment style.
* The teacher adjusts questions based on the students' learning level.
* Student responses are evaluated manually.
* Feedback is also written manually.
* The assessment workflow consumes a significant amount of the teacher's time.

---

# Pain Points

## 1. Assessment Creation

Preparing every question paper manually is repetitive and time-consuming.

Teachers often follow a consistent style, but they recreate assessments from scratch for every test.

---

## 2. Manual Evaluation

Every student response must be reviewed individually.

Descriptive answers require significant effort to evaluate consistently.

---

## 3. Feedback Generation

Providing meaningful feedback for every student increases the overall correction time.

---

# Existing Workflow Limitations

The current workflow has no dedicated assessment platform.

Most assessment-related activities are performed manually, including:

* Question paper preparation
* Assessment creation
* Answer evaluation
* Student feedback

As the number of students grows, the workload increases proportionally.

---

# Key Product Insights

The interview revealed several important product decisions.

## AI should assist—not replace—the teacher.

The teacher wants complete control over the final assessment and grading process.

---

## Assessments should match the teacher's style.

Teachers have their own way of writing questions.

Generic AI-generated questions are not sufficient.

The system should generate assessment drafts that resemble the teacher's wording, difficulty level, and assessment patterns.

---

## Human Review is Essential

Teachers must review:

* Generated question papers
* AI-generated marks
* Final feedback

No assessment or grade should be published automatically.

---

# Product Opportunities

Based on the interview, the following opportunities were identified:

* Reduce assessment creation time.
* Reduce grading time.
* Generate assessments in the teacher's own style.
* Support multiple answer formats.
* Provide AI-assisted grading with teacher approval.
* Improve consistency in student feedback.

---

# Out-of-Scope Requests

The customer did not identify the following as immediate priorities:

* Attendance management
* Fee management
* Parent portal
* WhatsApp integration

These features may be considered in future releases after validating the core assessment workflow.

---

# Research Outcomes

The interview confirmed that the primary problem is **not classroom management**.

The highest-value opportunity is improving the **assessment lifecycle**, specifically:

1. Creating assessments efficiently while preserving the teacher's style.
2. Evaluating student responses with AI assistance.
3. Keeping the teacher in control of all academic decisions.

These findings directly informed the product requirements and MVP scope for TutorFlow AI.
